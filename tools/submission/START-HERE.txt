Expenses - native macOS expense tracker with AI extraction and chat
Abdulghani Mikati, prepared for Magna AI, 17 November 2026

Requires macOS 15 or later.

1. Double-click Expenses.app. macOS will say it cannot verify the developer; click Done.
   Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to Expenses, then Open.
2. Press Cmd+, (Settings), paste the API key you received separately, click Save key.
3. Click Import and pick the PDFs in samples/ (the PNGs are Saudi receipt images to try too).
4. Ask the chat: "How much did I spend last month?"  "Show my spending by category for all time"
5. Tools menu > Evaluation... runs the accuracy tests.

README.pdf has the full guide. REPORT.pdf describes what was built, how, and the test results.
